# Art Generation & Asset Manifest (`十號風球 · Signal Ten`)

All art assets follow the **1962 Hong Kong cinema & mid-century gouache editorial illustration** direction (Wong Kar-wai *In the Mood for Love* / *Days of Being Wild* warm tungsten amber against slate-teal typhoon rain, 1950s–60s Hong Kong woodblock & gouache book illustration, visible paper grain, zero text/watermarks inside raw paintings).

## 1. Starter & Retouched Key Art (`assets/art/`)
- `assets/art/title_key.webp` (`3:4`, retouched from starter `title_key.jpg`): 1962 Royal Observatory Nathan Road hill looking toward Victoria Harbour in driving typhoon rain; the anachronistic red-black triangular flag on the iron signal mast was seamlessly removed and replaced with a painted 1962 Signal No. 10 cross shape (`十號颶風訊號`) hanging from the yardarm.
- `assets/art/cover.webp` (`780×1040`, `3:4`): Full-bleed poster key art with Traditional Chinese `十 號 風 球 / SIGNAL TEN · HONG KONG 1962` title typography in the upper sky safe zone.
- `assets/art/thumb.webp` (`390×520`, `3:4`): Downscaled poster thumbnail derived from `cover.webp`.
- `assets/art/desk.webp` (`3:4`): Overhead/three-quarter gouache painting of the 1962 Royal Observatory forecasting desk with Bakelite rotary telephone, brass barometer, divider calipers, and desk lamp.
- `assets/art/chart_base.webp` (`840×630`, `4:3`): 1962 Royal Observatory Hong Kong Form R.O. South China Sea Mercator synoptic plotting sheet (`14°N–25°N, 108°E–123°E`) showing Hong Kong, Waglan Island (`橫瀾島`), Macau (`澳門`), Pratas Island (`東沙島`), Paracel Islands (`西沙島`), Hainan (`海口`), Swatow (`汕頭`), Kaohsiung (`高雄`), Manila (`馬尼拉`), Luzon Strait, and 400 km / 800 km range rings.
